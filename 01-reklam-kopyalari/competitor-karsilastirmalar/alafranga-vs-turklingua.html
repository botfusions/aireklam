TL;DR Summary

Turklingua (since 1994) and Alafranga (since 2002) are both Turkish-focused, ISO-certified agencies. If your project is purely Turkish legal or regulatory, both qualify. If you need Turkish plus media, software, or a multilingual technical programme, Alafranga covers more ground.

Founded 1994 vs 2002 — Does It Matter?

Turklingua's 1994 founding gives it eight more years of history than Alafranga. In translation, that longevity signals stability and accumulated terminology — both genuine advantages. However, the relevant question is not just when a company was founded, but what it has done in that time and whether its operating model has kept pace with client needs.

Alafranga's track record includes documented multi-decade relationships (15 years with Hoogendoorn/LetsGrow, 13 years with Delta Plus), enterprise tech clients such as OpenAI, streaming credits with Disney+, and an active AI workflow consulting practice. For most industrial or technical buyers, this recent proof of delivery is more actionable than founding year alone.

Note on ISO 27001: Turklingua holds ISO 27001 (information security management), which Alafranga does not currently certify to this standard. If your documents include clinical trial data, financial records, or other highly sensitive material subject to data protection regulations, ISO 27001 certification is worth asking about from any provider you shortlist.

Turkish-Only vs Turkish-Core Multilingual

Turklingua's positioning is Turkish-only — this is their stated strength and competitive differentiator. For organisations whose entire language need is Turkish, that focus is valid. For organisations where Turkish is the primary pair but German, Dutch, French, or Arabic translations are needed alongside it, Turklingua's model creates a gap.

Alafranga's 40+ language portfolio was not built by catalogue decision — every language was added in response to real client demand. This means the multilingual capability sits alongside the Turkish core rather than replacing it. Clients running simultaneous Turkish + Dutch + German technical programmes report consistent terminology management across all pairs under one coordinator.

Media Translation — A Material Difference

Both agencies list subtitling services. But streaming platforms such as Disney+ apply strict technical and linguistic QA before accepting deliverables — timing, reading speed, subtitle line length, terminology consistency, and quality review all need to meet broadcast standards. Alafranga has passed those requirements, as evidenced by credited subtitle work for Disney+ and Fox TV.

If your content includes streaming localisation, broadcast subtitling, or multimedia requiring platform-grade review, Alafranga's media track record is a concrete differentiator.

AI Integration Consulting — A Service Turklingua Doesn't Offer

Alafranga offers AI Integration Consulting as a distinct service: designing AI translation workflows, implementing platforms, and building custom CMS/ERP integrations based on their SmartEdit production experience. This is useful for organisations that want to bring AI-assisted translation in-house or integrate a vendor's workflow into their own systems.

Turklingua explicitly states they deliver no machine-generated drafts — which signals a commitment to human quality but means they do not offer AI workflow consulting. Whether this is an advantage or limitation depends entirely on what you need: maximum human oversight, or a hybrid AI+human system built to your infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Both have ISO 17100 — how do I choose?

ISO 17100 defines the process; it does not guarantee identical output quality. Look at the audit body (Alafranga: Bureau Veritas), the range of clients served, documented case studies, and the specific sector depth relevant to your content. Both agencies are legitimate ISO 17100 holders — the decision rests on fit for your content type and programme structure.

Can Alafranga handle legal and regulatory content?

Yes. Alafranga's service portfolio includes legal translation and compliance documentation. Their industry pages cover Compliance, Safety & Standards, Energy & Power Systems, and Automotive & Mobility — all areas with significant regulatory translation requirements. Turklingua's legal and pharma depth is more explicitly marketed, but Alafranga's track record includes legal and industrial compliance content.

Which agency is better for a new long-term programme?

Both agencies are capable of managing long-term programmes. Alafranga's public case studies — 15 years with a horticulture technology group, 13 years with a PPE manufacturer — provide verified evidence of multi-decade programme continuity. If demonstrable programme longevity is a procurement criterion, Alafranga's documented history is directly relevant.

Can Alafranga match Turklingua's Turkish-only depth?

Alafranga's founding pair is Turkish. The agency operated exclusively as a Turkish translation office from 2002 until expanding into other languages in 2006. The Turkish language capability is not a catalogue addition — it is the foundation of the business. For the majority of Turkish technical, legal, and media content, depth is comparable.
