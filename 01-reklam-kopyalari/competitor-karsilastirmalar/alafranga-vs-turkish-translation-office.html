Alafranga is a founder-led technical specialist active since 2002, built around Turkish and industrial/engineering content — with direct accountability on every project. Founding Year and Track Record

Alafranga was founded in Istanbul in 2002 — five years before Turkish Translation Office opened. Those extra five years mean more documented project history, longer relationships with specific industrial clients, and a more established terminology database. TTO launched in 2008 and has grown into a sizeable agency, but the earlier start gives Alafranga a meaningful head start in accumulated technical translation experience.

Alafranga has been an ATC (Association of Translation Companies) member since 2007, and joined EUATC at the same time — in both cases, before TTO existed. Industry membership at this level involves vetting of quality systems, professional conduct, and financial stability, making it a meaningful differentiator for clients sourcing from regulated sectors.

Specialist vs General Agency Model

The most important structural difference between these two agencies is scope. TTO operates as a general language services platform across many languages and service types — document translation, literary translation, sworn translation, multimedia, DTP, digital marketing, and more. This breadth serves clients who need one vendor for everything.

Alafranga made the opposite choice: Turkish is the founding pair and the core of the business. Every other language in the 40+ portfolio was added because a real client with a real need asked for it — not because a market trend suggested it. For companies where Turkish terminology consistency matters most, this focus translates into fewer errors and shorter review cycles.

Founder-Led Delivery vs Agency Platform

TTO uses an online ordering system and automated pricing — useful for speed and volume, but it means your project enters a workflow queue rather than being reviewed personally. For technical content where a miscommunicated specification or a wrong unit of measurement has real downstream consequences, the automated path introduces risk.

At Alafranga, every project is reviewed by a coordinator. There are no handovers mid-project. The same team that starts your engineering manual is the team that finishes it. This continuity is what makes 13-year and 15-year client relationships possible.

Media Translation Credits

If your content includes subtitling, streaming localisation, or broadcast media, Alafranga's credentials stand apart from TTO. Alafranga has delivered subtitle localisation for Disney+ and Fox TV — broadcast and streaming platforms that apply strict QA requirements before accepting subtitles. TTO offers multimedia services but does not cite equivalent credits publicly.

AI and Technology Integration

Both agencies have invested in technology. TTO offers an AI-based machine translation platform and client portal for project tracking. Alafranga's approach is different: their SmartEdit workflow uses AI-assisted drafts reviewed by human specialists inside MemoQ and SDL Trados, with three defined quality tiers — all ISO 17100:2015 certified. Alafranga also offers AI Integration Consulting as a standalone service, meaning they can help clients build Turkish-language AI workflows inside their own CMS or ERP systems — a service TTO does not appear to offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Alafranga offer the same language coverage as Turkish Translation Office?

Alafranga works in 40+ languages. TTO's platform covers more language pairs in its catalogue. If Turkish is your primary requirement and other languages are secondary, Alafranga's depth in the Turkish pair outweighs TTO's breadth. If you need ten languages simultaneously with no particular Turkish focus, TTO's platform model may be more practical.

Which agency is faster for urgent work?

TTO's online portal allows instant order placement, which speeds up the initial step. Alafranga offers a dedicated "Translation Under Pressure" service for regulatory submissions, product launches, and legal deadlines — with a project manager who reviews feasibility before committing. For genuinely high-stakes urgent content, human review of the brief is safer than automated acceptance.

How does Alafranga's ISO certification compare?

Both agencies hold ISO 17100:2015. Alafranga's certification is audited by Bureau Veritas — one of the largest and most recognised certification bodies in the world. The audit body matters: Bureau Veritas certifications carry weight with procurement departments in industrial, energy, and regulated sectors.

Is there a migration path if we switch from TTO to Alafranga?

Yes. Alafranga's team can work from existing glossaries, style guides, and translation memories. Provided your previous agency can export your TM in standard format (TMX), Alafranga can import and build on your prior work rather than starting from scratch.
